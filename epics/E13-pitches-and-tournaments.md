# E13 - Pitches and tournaments

## What is wanted

Turn the app into something pitch owners use: owners and managers of pitches,
tournament organisers, paid through advertising or a price.

## Why

It is the product's business model.

## What is already decided

- A pitch and a tournament are contexts with their own members tables and roles (ADR
  0002 section 3); a tournament may become a rating scope (ADR 0002 section 9).
